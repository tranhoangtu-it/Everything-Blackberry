## 🌐 Primary Communities

### CrackBerry — The Definitive BlackBerry Forum

**URL**: `https://forums.crackberry.com`

The largest and most active English-language BlackBerry community, founded in 2004 by Kevin Michaluk. After BB's decline, CrackBerry pivoted to covering all smartphone news, but the BlackBerry forums remain active archives.

**Best subforums for BB10 users:**
- `forums.crackberry.com/blackberry-10-os-f295/` — BB10 General
- `forums.crackberry.com/blackberry-10-os-apps-f323/` — BB10 Apps
- `forums.crackberry.com/blackberry-10-os-development-f395/` — BB10 Development
- `forums.crackberry.com/blackberry-classic-q20-f428/` — Classic-specific
- `forums.crackberry.com/blackberry-passport-f429/` — Passport-specific

**What you'll find**:
- Pinned threads with autoloader downloads and checksums
- Community app packages (`.bar` files)
- Troubleshooting guides from experienced users
- Device-specific tips and tricks

## 🇯🇵 Japanese Tech Perspective — Zenn.dev

**URL**: `https://zenn.dev`

Zenn is a Japanese tech writing platform popular with Japanese software developers. A surprising amount of BlackBerry-related technical content is published here by Japanese BB enthusiasts, who represent one of the most active non-English BB communities.

**Notable Zenn content categories to search:**
- `BlackBerry 10` — Technical articles on BB10 development
- `QNX` — QNX embedded development articles
- `Cascades` — Cascades UI framework tutorials

**Why the Japanese community matters:**
Japan maintained one of the largest BB user bases outside North America, partly due to the popularity of BBM before iMessage and LINE dominated. Japanese developers contributed several community tools and patches.

## 🔒 Security & Privacy Notes

> BlackBerry devices were known for exceptional security in the corporate era. In 2026, the security posture is different:

| Aspect | Status | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **BES/BES12 encryption** | ❌ Servers offline | Enterprise push encryption no longer available |
| **QNX kernel security** | ✅ Still excellent | The OS itself remains highly secure |
| **PIN-based device encryption** | ✅ Still works | On-device AES encryption is intact |
| **OS security patches** | ❌ Ended Jan 2022 | BB10 receives no new security updates |
| **App security** | ⚠️ Audit sideloaded apps | No Play Protect or App Store scanning |
| **Network security** | ✅ Use VPN | OpenVPN Connect works on BB10 |
